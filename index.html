04 / RESULTS

Process metrics

CPU utilization — busy time / elapsed time

AT = Arrival Time · BT = Burst Time · ST = First Start Time · CT = Completion Time · TAT = Turnaround Time · WT = Waiting Time · RT = Response Time

Calculation rules
  • TAT = CT − AT
  • WT = TAT − BT
  • RT = ST − AT
  • CPU utilization = total busy time ÷ elapsed time × 100%
